SMS Terms & Privacy

Terms of service and privacy policy for our text messaging.

Effective August 2026

SMS Terms of Service

Dom's Mobile AutoWorks ("we," "us," or "our") offers text messaging so we can communicate with customers about their vehicles and appointments. By providing your mobile number and opting in, you agree to the terms below.

Program & message types

Once you opt in, we may send you text messages related to: appointment scheduling and confirmations, service and status updates, price quotes and invoices, and replies to your customer-support questions. This is a conversational messaging program — we do not send recurring marketing blasts.

How you opt in

You opt in by texting us first, by using the "Text to Book" links on our website, or by giving us your number when you request service. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies and depends on your interactions with us — for example, when you book a visit, ask a question, or we send you an update about a job.

Message & data rates

Message and data rates may apply for any messages sent to you from us and to us from you, depending on your mobile carrier and plan. If you have questions about your text or data plan, contact your wireless provider.

How to opt out

You can cancel at any time by replying STOP. After you send STOP, we'll send one confirmation message and then stop texting you. If you'd like to join again, just text us as you did the first time and we'll resume.

Get help

If you're having trouble with the messaging program, reply HELP for assistance, or reach us directly anytime.

Carrier disclaimer

Carriers (your wireless provider and others) are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy Policy

Dom's Mobile AutoWorks respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you contact us or book service, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, service or home address, and vehicle information (year, make, model, and the service you need).

How we use your information

We use your information only to schedule and perform your service, provide quotes and invoices, communicate with you about your vehicle, and answer your questions.

Text messaging & opt-in data

We do not share your mobile number, text-messaging opt-in data, or consent with any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except the messaging aggregators and providers that help us deliver the text messages — and only for the purpose of delivering those messages.

No sale of your data

We do not sell your personal information to anyone. We only share information when it's necessary to complete the service you requested (for example, ordering the correct parts for your vehicle) or when required by law.

Questions

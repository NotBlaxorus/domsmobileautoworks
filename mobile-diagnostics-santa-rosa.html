Diagnosis before replacement

A car that clicks, cranks without starting, or has a battery warning can have different underlying problems. I check the symptoms and test relevant systems before recommending parts. Depending on the vehicle, that can include battery and connection checks, starting and charging tests, trouble codes and live diagnostic data.

What to send when your car won’t start

Text the year, make, model and engine if you know it, plus your city and where the vehicle is parked. Tell me whether it cranks, clicks or makes no sound, which warning lights are on, and whether the problem started suddenly or comes and goes. If you have already tried a jump-start or replaced parts, include that too.

What happens after the diagnosis?

I’ll explain the findings and discuss the next step. Many battery, starter, alternator and other repairs can be done on-site, but access, the vehicle and the work required determine what is practical. Diagnostic work and repairs are discussed directly with you before booking; there is no automatic online estimate.

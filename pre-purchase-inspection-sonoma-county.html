A closer look before you buy

A pre-purchase inspection can help you understand the accessible mechanical condition of a used car. We’ll agree on the scope before booking. Depending on access and the vehicle, the inspection can include visible components, signs of leaks, starting and charging concerns, and diagnostic checks where supported.

Arrange access with the seller

Send the vehicle’s year, make, model, engine if known and location, along with any concerns from the listing or seller. Confirm that the seller agrees to an inspection and that there is a suitable place to work. A road test or other checks depend on permission and what can safely be arranged.

Know the limits of an on-site inspection

An inspection reports findings from the checks agreed upon and accessible at the time. It cannot guarantee future reliability or reveal every hidden or intermittent problem. Some issues need a lift, further testing or disassembly beyond a mobile visit. I’ll discuss what can be checked before we schedule.
